Sources & caveats

Compiled 21 Sep 2026 (extended through Dec 2027) from RunningCalendar, GoTrail, EntryNinja, Otter, Oyster Catcher, UTCT, WannaDo/Tower-2-Sea, Cape Cobra, Energy Events, Chapman’s Peak, Two Oceans, Sanlam Cape Town Marathon, MUT by UTMB, Knysna Forest Marathon, and club listings. Dates marked provisional/TBA are estimated from prior years and must be confirmed with organisers. Entry status changes quickly.

Images: race posters are organisers’ event artwork (og:image / cover) saved for offline use with a credit link — not redistributed as our own. Where no poster was available, a freely licensed destination photo (Wikimedia Commons) is shown. Prefer the official site for current branding.

T-shirt badges: included = tee in the entry fee; add-on = optional paid shirt; no tee listed = organiser published merch/handouts without a shirt; shirt? = not stated — do not assume a shirt. Prefer the official entry form.

Map markers are approximate town centroids along the Cape Town–Garden Route–Gqeberha corridor (Leaflet + OpenStreetMap tiles). Multiple races in the same town share a marker with weekend-grouped popups and official entry links.

Personal race status (interested / entered / done) is stored only in this browser via localStorage — it is not synced to a server.

Window runs through 31 Dec 2027. Gun Run 2026 was 12–13 Sep (just before the window start) and is excluded; a provisional mid-Sep 2027 Gun Run is listed. Many spring/summer 2027 dates remain provisional until organisers publish — reconfirm before travelling.

Gold edge / gold ring = near George (Mossel Bay–Knysna–Plett corridor) · Parkruns and cycling-only events excluded
