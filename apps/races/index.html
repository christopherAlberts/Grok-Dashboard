Sources & caveats

Compiled 14 Sep 2026 from RunningCalendar, GoTrail, EntryNinja, Otter, Oyster Catcher, UTCT, WannaDo/Tower-2-Sea, Cape Cobra, Energy Events, Chapman’s Peak, and club listings. Dates marked TBA are estimated from prior years and must be confirmed with organisers. Entry status changes quickly.

Images: race posters are organisers’ event artwork (og:image / cover) saved for offline use with a credit link — not redistributed as our own. Where no poster was available, a freely licensed destination photo (Wikimedia Commons) is shown. Prefer the official site for current branding.

T-shirt badges: included = tee in the entry fee; add-on = optional paid shirt; no tee listed = organiser published merch/handouts without a shirt; shirt? = not stated — do not assume a shirt. Prefer the official entry form.

Map markers are approximate town centroids along the Cape Town–Garden Route–Gqeberha corridor (Leaflet + OpenStreetMap tiles). Multiple races in the same town share a marker with a combined popup.

Outside this window (so not listed): Two Oceans (Mar/Apr 2027), Sanlam Cape Town Marathon (May 2027), MUT by UTMB George (May 2027), Knysna Forest Marathon (July). Gun Run was 12–13 Sep 2026, just before the window.

Gold edge / gold ring = near George (Mossel Bay–Knysna–Plett corridor) · Parkruns and cycling-only events excluded
